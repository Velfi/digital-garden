# Welcome to Kaleidopaint

Kaleidopaint is a drawing app that mirrors your strokes into kaleidoscope patterns.

## Quick Start

1. Pick a color in the **Color** tab.
2. In **Draw**, keep **Paint** selected.
3. Click and drag on the canvas to draw.
4. Open **Symmetry** and change:
   - **Mode** (Polar or Mosaic)
   - **Folds** (more folds = denser pattern)
5. Use **Cmd/Ctrl + Z** to undo mistakes.
6. Export your art from **File -> Export PNG**.

## Most Useful Controls

- **Left mouse drag**: paint
- **Right mouse drag**: erase
- **Mouse wheel**: zoom in/out
- **Space + drag** (or middle mouse drag): pan canvas
- **Origin tool**: click to move symmetry center
- **Rotate tool**: drag to rotate symmetry

## Good First Workflow

1. Start with **Polar** symmetry and 6-8 folds.
2. Paint broad strokes with a larger brush size.
3. Add detail with a smaller brush.
4. Move the symmetry origin for more complex designs.
5. Try **Mosaic** mode for geometric tiling-style patterns.

## Saving

- Kaleidopaint auto-saves your latest canvas in this browser.
- **New canvas** creates a blank canvas size you choose.
- **Export PNG** downloads your artwork as an image file.
