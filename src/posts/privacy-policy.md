# {title}

My apps don't require or ask for personal information and no information you enter will be transmitted to me or to any third party.

Game progress is stored locally in the app sandbox on your device. 

**Game Center:** Achievement progress is synced through Apple’s Game Center service under your Apple ID, subject to [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**Network:** The app may connect to the internet only for Game Center. No gameplay data is uploaded to our servers.

**Contact:** [support@zeldas.page](mailto:support@zeldas.page) for privacy questions.
