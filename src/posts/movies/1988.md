Earlier in 2025, my wife and I watched most movies from 1987 that got a wide release in theaters. We skipped several movies, mostly ones starring transphobes or ones about things that really depressed us. We started with the smallest box office movies and worked our way up.

We're going to do the same for 1988. For every movie we watch, I'll write a short review and give it a rating:

- I didn't watch it
- Not worth watching
- Worth watching once
- Worth watching again and again

## The movies, in the order that we watched them

### Buster

**Buster** is a British romantic crime comedy-drama directed by David Green, starring Phil Collins as Ronald “Buster” Edwards and Julie Walters as his wife, June. The film is based on the true story of one of the participants in the infamous [1963 Great Train Robbery].

- I wasn't immediately warm to this movie, but it grew on me.
- I liked how the plot focused on the thief after he's 'run away to paradise' and shows how he misses life before the big job.

- The two Phil Collins songs in the movie won some awards.

_Worth watching once_

[1963 Great Train Robbery]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Train_Robbery_(1963)

### Spellbinder

**Spellbinder** is an American supernatural thriller directed by Janet Greek, starring Timothy Daly as Jeff Mills and Kelly Preston as Miranda Reed. The film explores themes of deception, the occult, and betrayal, set against the backdrop of late-1980s Los Angeles.

A satanic panic witchcraft movie; This one felt like an X-Files episode.

- It reminded me of last year's [The Believers].
- This movie is the first time I've had the pleasure of seeing [Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa] play a
  character that's not a yellow-peril villain. I wish he had been cast in more things. His part in this movie is small.
- Basil Poledouris did an Angelo Badalamenti, but not a very good one.
- Had a cool part where the two leads are beset by a coven of witches. I liked the way they bent the windows.
- They draw a circle of protection. I'm reminded of a similar scene in [The Devil Rides Out].

[The Believers]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Believers_(film)
[Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cary-Hiroyuki_Tagawa
[The Devil Rides Out]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Devil_Rides_Out_(film)

_Worth watching once_

### Beaches 

**Beaches** is an American musical comedy-drama directed by Garry Marshall, starring Bette Midler as Cecilia Carol "C.C." Bloom and Barbara Hershey as Hillary Whitney Essex. Based on Iris Rainer Dart’s 1985 novel, the film explores the enduring friendship between two women from different backgrounds over several decades.

- Did they really used to have child revues in Atlantic City? So weird.
- I didn't like the characters enough for this one to work.
- Roger Ebert said it: "'Beaches' lacks the spontaneity of life. This is a movie completely constructed out of other movies - out of cliches and archetypes that were old before most of the cast members were born."
- It was nice to see [Spalding Gray].

[Spalding Gray]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spalding_Gray

_Not worth watching_

### Maniac Cop

**Maniac Cop** is a horror-crime film directed by William Lustig starring Bruce Campbell, Tom Atkins, Laurene Landon, and Robert Z'Dar. It follows a wave of murders in New York City committed by a uniformed officer.

- I wish [Bruce Campbell] had been in more stuff.
- The maniac cop makeup was pretty good/gruesome.

_Worth watching once_

[Bruce Campbell]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Campbell

### And God Created Woman

**And God Created Woman** is an American comedy-drama directed by Roger Vadim. The film stars Rebecca De Mornay as Robin Shea, a convict who marries carpenter Billy Moran (Vincent Spano) to secure early parole. Post-release, Robin pursues a music career and becomes involved with politician James Tiernan (Frank Langella), leading to complications.

- I don't understand how the marriage allowed this woman to get out of prison.
- It's a very strange movie. Ashley compared it to [Citizen Ruth].
- This woman is insane.
- I'm always happy to see [Frank Langella]. Wait, not like this.
- My goodness, these sex scenes are excessive.

_Worth watching once?_

[Frank Langella]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Langella
[Citizen Ruth]: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizen_Ruth
